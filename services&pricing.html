Good to know

A few quick answers.

How do the service prices work?

Prices shown are recommended starting rates. We’ll confirm the scope and final price with you before any work begins.

Are hardware and software licences included?

Hardware, software licences, and any travel charges are quoted separately where applicable.

Can I get help remotely?

Yes. Remote desktop support is available for supported computer issues, and you stay in control of your device throughout the session.

How is remote desktop support billed?

The starting rate is R350 per hour, billed in 30-minute increments. We’ll confirm the expected work and price with you before starting.

How do I request a service?

Select “Book this service” on a service card to prepare a request, or contact CompuSoft by phone, WhatsApp, or the contact form.

What should I include when asking for help?

Tell us which device or service is affected, what you see, and when the issue began. You can use the contact form even if you’re unsure which service to choose. Don’t include passwords or one-time codes.

These are recommended starting rates, not a final quote. Final cost depends on the time and complexity involved. Hardware, software licences, and any travel charges are quoted separately where applicable.
