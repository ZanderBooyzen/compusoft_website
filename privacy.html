Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This notice explains what information CompuSoft receives through this website, why it is used, and which service providers help operate the site. If anything is unclear, email mail@compusoft.co.za.

Support requests

The support form asks for your first name, an optional surname, at least one way to contact you (email or phone), and a description of the computer problem. Please do not include passwords, one-time codes, banking information, or other secrets.

When you submit the form, the information is saved as an inquiry in the website’s Cloudflare D1 database and sent to CompuSoft by email so the request can be answered. The email is delivered to the CompuSoft business inbox. The information is used to understand the issue, contact you, discuss next steps, and keep a record of the enquiry.

Product quote list

The older product quote-list page can save the product names, quantities, item notes, and additional notes you enter. That quote-list request is stored as an inquiry and does not include your name, email address, or phone number. Use the support form or the listed contact methods separately if you want CompuSoft to contact you about it.

Storage and retention

Inquiry information is held in the website’s Cloudflare database and in the business email account. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule configured for these enquiries. Records may remain until they are manually removed or are no longer needed to handle the request and maintain business records.

You can email mail@compusoft.co.za to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted. Some records may need to be kept for legitimate business record-keeping purposes.

Service providers and external links

Cloudflare provides website hosting, form processing, the inquiry database, and email delivery infrastructure. Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on this page; your browser requests those font files from Google. The service-area map link opens Google Maps only if you choose to follow it. Phone, email, and WhatsApp links open the corresponding app or service when selected.

These providers may process technical information such as your IP address and browser request data to deliver and protect their services. Their handling is described in their own notices: Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and analytics

The CompuSoft website code does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Cloudflare may process technical request and diagnostic information as part of hosting and protecting the website.

Updates

This notice may be updated if the website’s forms, providers, or information handling change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
